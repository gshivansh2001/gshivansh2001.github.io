Research

I am interested in how creators and users respond to the rules that govern digital platforms, such as content moderation, disclosure mandates, and privacy protections, and why well-intended policies often produce outcomes their designers did not anticipate. In my work so far, I have used large-scale platform data, LLM-based measurement validated against human annotation, and causal inference methods, along with field and survey experiments.
